# Small Business Administration (SBA) - APIs.json Profile

APIs.json provider profile for the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA), covering public REST APIs for SBA loan data, business licensing, disaster loan applications, government contracting, and small business certification programs.

## APIs Covered

- **SBA.gov Content API** - Search SBA.gov content including lenders, articles, and documents
- **CAFS Loan Origination API** - Capital Access Financial System for SBA 7(a) loan origination and servicing
- **PPP Loan Forgiveness API** - Paycheck Protection Program forgiveness processing
- **SBIR/STTR Awards and Solicitations API** - Innovation research award and solicitation data
- **SBA Open Data API** - CKAN-based open data portal with size standards and loan datasets
