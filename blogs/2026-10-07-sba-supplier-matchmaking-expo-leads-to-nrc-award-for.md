A connection at SBA’s Supplier Matchmaking Expo in Detroit led to a procurement award for RJ’s Printing, a family-owned small business in Kalamazoo, Michigan. The company will provide new event signage, visual displays, and supply chain materials for the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission’s external engagement efforts.
